# Natural Traditional Chinese for Taiwan

Write for the intended audience and setting, as if the piece were first drafted in Chinese in Taiwan. When working from English, preserve the meaning, facts, certainty, and technical constraints, but choose the Chinese phrasing and sentence order freely.

Use the level of formality and mix of Chinese and English that fit the context. Keep official names, project terms, identifiers, filenames, and quoted text accurate; do not force translations or follow a fixed list of preferred words.

Read the result as standalone Chinese before returning it. Revise wording that sounds stiff, translated, or out of place for its readers.
